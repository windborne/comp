---
title: 'Single Sign-On (OIDC)'
description: 'Let your team sign in to Comp AI and the employee portal with your identity provider'
---

Comp AI supports single sign-on (SSO) through **OpenID Connect (OIDC)**, so your team can sign in to
the compliance app and the employee portal with the identity provider you already use — Okta,
Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, JumpCloud, OneLogin, Keycloak, and any other OIDC-compliant
provider.

## How it works

- An organization admin registers the identity provider under **Settings → Single sign-on** and
  proves ownership of the organization's email domain with a DNS record.
- People pick **Continue with single sign-on** on the app or portal login page and enter their work
  email. The email domain selects the provider, so the same flow serves both apps.
- People who don't have a Comp AI account yet are created on first sign-in and added to your
  organization with the **employee** role, which gives them access to the employee portal (policies,
  training, device agent). Admins grant app access from the **People** page as usual.
- People who already have a Comp AI account (for example, they previously signed in with Google) are
  linked to the provider on their first SSO sign-in — as long as their email is under your verified
  domain.

<Note>
  Single sign-on only adds people through SSO sign-ins. Signing in with Google or Microsoft using an
  email under your domain does not add anyone to your organization.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- You are an **owner** or **admin** of the organization in Comp AI.
- You can create an OIDC application in your identity provider.
- You can add a DNS TXT record for your email domain.

## Set up single sign-on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an OIDC application in your identity provider">
    Create a **Web** application using the **Authorization Code** flow. Configure:

    - **Redirect / sign-in URI**: `https://<your-api-host>/api/auth/sso/callback/<provider-id>`. The
      exact value is shown in Comp AI once you pick a provider ID (for example
      `https://api.trycomp.ai/api/auth/sso/callback/acme`).
    - **Scopes**: `openid`, `profile`, `email`.
    - **Grant type**: authorization code, with PKCE where available.

    Note the **issuer URL**, **client ID** and **client secret** the provider gives you.

    | Provider | Issuer URL |
    | --- | --- |
    | Okta | `https://<your-org>.okta.com` (or your custom authorization server) |
    | Microsoft Entra ID | `https://login.microsoftonline.com/<tenant-id>/v2.0` |
    | Google Workspace | `https://accounts.google.com` |
    | Keycloak | `https://<host>/realms/<realm>` |

  </Step>

  <Step title="Register the provider in Comp AI">
    In the app, open **Settings → Single sign-on** and click **Add provider**. Enter:

    - **Provider ID** — a short slug such as `acme`. It becomes part of the redirect URI and the DNS
      record and cannot be changed later.
    - **Issuer URL** — Comp AI fetches the provider's discovery document
      (`/.well-known/openid-configuration`) to validate the configuration.
    - **Email domain** — the domain of your team's work emails (for example `acme.com`). Separate
      several domains with commas.
    - **Client ID** and **Client secret** from step 1.

    The client secret is stored for the token exchange and never shown again.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email domain">
    After saving, Comp AI shows a DNS **TXT** record:

    ```
    Name:  _compai-sso-<provider-id>.<your-domain>
    Value: <verification token>
    ```

    Add it at your DNS provider, wait for it to propagate (usually minutes, up to an hour), then
    click **Verify domain** on the provider card. If you registered several domains, add the same
    record under each of them.

    <Warning>
      Sign-in through the provider stays disabled until the domain is verified. Verification is
      what prevents someone else from registering your domain and signing in as your users.
    </Warning>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in">
    On the app or portal login page, choose **Continue with single sign-on**, enter a work email
    under the verified domain and complete sign-in with your identity provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security model

- **Domain ownership is required.** A provider can only sign people in once its domain is verified,
  and only for email addresses under that domain. Identities the provider asserts for other domains
  are rejected before any account is created or linked.
- **Existing accounts are linked safely.** An SSO identity is linked to an existing Comp AI account
  only when the email matches the provider's verified domain.
- **Deactivated members stay deactivated.** Signing in through SSO never re-activates an offboarded
  member.
- **Secrets are never returned.** The API omits the client secret from every response and redacts
  it from audit logs.

## Removing a provider

Removing a provider deletes it together with the SSO links of everyone who signed in through it.
People keep their Comp AI accounts and memberships and can sign in with another method (magic link,
Google, Microsoft) or through a newly registered provider.

## Troubleshooting

| Message on the login page                                                                    | What it means                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No single sign-on provider is set up for this email domain                                   | No verified provider matches the email domain. Check the domain on the provider card.                                                         |
| Single sign-on for this email domain has not been verified yet                               | Add the DNS TXT record and click **Verify domain**.                                                                                           |
| This email already has a Comp AI account that cannot be linked…                              | The existing account's email is not under the provider's verified domain, or has never been verified. Sign in with the previous method first. |
| Your identity provider returned an email address outside your organization's verified domain | The provider asserted an email for another domain. Check the user's primary email in the provider.                                            |
| Your identity provider returned an error                                                     | The token exchange failed. Confirm the redirect URI, client ID and secret in the provider match Comp AI.                                      |

## Self-hosting notes

- The **redirect URI** is served by the API (`BASE_URL`), so the API host must be reachable from
  users' browsers and the API must be able to reach the identity provider.
- Identity providers on **private networks** are rejected by default. Add the provider's origin to
  `AUTH_TRUSTED_ORIGINS` on the API to allow it.
- Set `PORTAL_DISABLE_SSO_SIGN_IN=true` on the portal to hide the single sign-on option there.
